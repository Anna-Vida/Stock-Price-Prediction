MAE = mean absolute error in price units; lower is better. The baseline assumes no price change. Direction accuracy is a historical measurement, not a probability of a future gain. RMSE emphasizes larger errors; MAPE expresses error as a percentage. Band coverage is the measured percentage of outcomes inside the model bands. Overlapping samples are not independent.
